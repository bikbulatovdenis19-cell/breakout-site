Privacy Policy

A clear pre-launch privacy statement for the BreakOut website. This page will be expanded before accounts, payments and commercial licensing are enabled.

Version: 19 August 2026 · Pre-launch
The current GitHub Pages preview does not include account forms, payment processing, advertising pixels or analytics SDKs. Hosting infrastructure may still process ordinary technical request data such as IP address and browser information for security and delivery.

1. What BreakOut currently collects

On this pre-launch website, BreakOut does not intentionally collect account credentials, payment card details, match video, project files or analytics events. If you contact support by email, the information you send is processed only for the purpose of handling your request.

2. What will change at commercial launch

When accounts and licensing are enabled, BreakOut may process the minimum information required to provide the service, such as email address, account identifier, subscription state, license entitlement, activated device identifiers and security logs. The policy will be updated before those features are made public.

3. Payments

BreakOut is designed so that payment card data is handled by the selected payment provider rather than stored directly by the BreakOut website or application. The final payment provider and its privacy information will be listed here before sales begin.

4. Video and project content

The desktop application is designed around local professional analysis. Any future cloud or synchronization features will be described separately, including exactly what is uploaded, why, and how users control it.

5. Security and retention

Production account and license systems will use access controls, encrypted transport and limited data retention appropriate to the service. Secrets and private signing keys will never be stored in the public GitHub Pages repository.

6. Your choices

Before commercial launch, the website will provide clear routes for account access, support, account deletion where applicable, and privacy requests.

7. Contact

Privacy questions can be sent to support.
